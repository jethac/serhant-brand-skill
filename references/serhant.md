# Serhant frameworks

Distilled from Ryan Serhant, *Brand It Like Serhant* (Hachette Go, 2024). This file is the source of truth for the system. `SKILL.md` is the procedure.

## Foundation

Brand is the foundation. If they do not control it, the market, the employer, or the comments will brand them.

## Phase 1 — core identity

**Formula V:** VIEWPOINT + VALUES = BRAND VISION STATEMENT.

The statement is 3–5 years out, aspirational but doable, simple, heart-driven, for them alone (not a tattoo), worth fighting for.

Headline exercise: local magazine cover in five years — headline, keywords, clients, value delivered.

Template: *Be [region]'s go-to [category] for [special niche] by [timeframe].*

**The AND:** *I am [profession] AND [unexpected pairing].* The pairing is the differentiator, not the skill. People do not like being sold to; they love shopping with friends.

Interest audit (find the AND from commonalities):

1. Where does the mind wander away from work?
2. Two extra hours a day — how spent?
3. A recent experience so fun they had to repeat it
4. When they demonstrated a skill, and how people responded
5. Job if money were irrelevant
6. Greatest non-work accomplishments
7. What people constantly ask them about

Then: Communicate, Connect, Learn with that AND.

**ESP:** Emphasize brand image. Speak to the customer they want. Perfect the voice.

**Three power adjectives.** Ban baseline words: dependable, hardworking, honest, passionate, authentic, professional. They must map to the AND. Test every post: *does this represent all three?* If no, delete.

Zone of authenticity = self-descriptors ∩ words from clients/colleagues.

**Visuals last.** Color, typeface, logo after identity. Style guide. Own-domain email. Headshots and lifestyle photos prove the vision in the wild. Five authenticity checks: anyone embarrassed, internet-forever, a lie, related to core, overdoing it.

## Phase 2 — consistency

**Five Families:** Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn. X/Threads as honorees. Engagement over follower count. Never buy followers.

**Content matrix:** 3 characters (sides customers see) × 3 offerings (Entertain / Educate / Encourage). Plot cells, then a calendar, then volume. Repurpose.

**Video / post formula:** (1) tactical points with numbers (2) personal story (3) CTA. Hearable voice, visible face, clear visuals.

**KPIs:** reach, engagement, conversion. Early target ~10% engagement.

**Three connectors** (pick a primary): Punch Line; Let Me Solve Your Problems; Inspire on Demand. ABC — Always Be Captivating. Good is the friend; volume beats preciousness.

**On/Off table.** Safe-ish: money, relationships, health. Caution: politics, religion, sex. Four killers: the lie, the vent, the faux expert, the call-out.

## Phase 3 — shout

**3 C's**

- Credibility: thought leadership from the AND (outlier, predict, unique-fact, rule of five); speaking; awards (nominate yourself)
- Coverage: press kit, media list, individualized pitches. Chase the Big Unlock. Publicist as stamp
- Community: GLEE — Gather, Listen, Engage, Evolve. Audience = spoken *to*; community = spoken *with*

**Partnerships.** Three tests: values, strengths, audience happy. Hell yes or no. Get your own eggs first (proof of value/reach), then ride coattails, later share them.

**Transformation.** Know what the brand is AND is not (audience will brand you as the obvious product). Mistakes are data. Ask for the moon.

## Quick-start order

1. Formula V
2. Interest audit → AND
3. Three adjectives via Zone of Authenticity
4. Content matrix
5. Calendar + volume
6. Credibility → Coverage → Community
7. Partnerships only after vetting
